Rules at a glance
A = 12–10 face valueJ Q K = 10Red K = −1Joker = 0
  • Everyone gets 4 face-down cards in a square and may look at their bottom two once.
  • On your turn, draw from the deck or take the top discard. Then swap it with one of your cards (the old one is discarded unseen) or discard it.
  • Discarding a drawn card uses its power: 7–8 look at one of yours, 9–10 look at an opponent's, J blind-swap any two, Q swap one of yours with another and see what you got, K look at any two and choose whether to swap.
  • Snap: when the top discard matches a card on the table, tap Snap and then that card. A wrong card costs you a penalty card. Snap someone else's card and you may hand them one of yours.
  • Call KABOO at the start of your turn when you think you have 3 or fewer points. Everyone else gets one last turn and may call Counter-KABOO instead to try to beat you. Running out of cards calls KABOO automatically.
  • Lowest total wins and scores 0. Everyone else scores their cards. A KABOO or Counter-KABOO caller who doesn't win takes +20. A caller who only ties does not win.
